What you’re watching

Plan once. Learn a continuous controller.

01

Plan

Inflate each obstacle by the robot’s safety margin, then run A* and shortcut only collision-free segments.

02

Demonstrate

Sample time-indexed positions and velocities along the safe route, with small variations inside the start and goal regions.

03

Roll out

Train the MLP to predict that velocity field, then integrate it with Euler steps from unseen starting points.
