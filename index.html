Plan

Enclose the workspace, keep the safety boundary infeasible, and give A* a logarithmic barrier cost that rises near obstacles and walls.

02

Pair

Pair free-space sources with samples along the full route. Turn on progress s to rank sources from start-nearest to goal-nearest.

03

Match

Integrate over flow time τ to recover the path distribution. With progress enabled, each particle also targets its ordered route position.
